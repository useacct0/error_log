Setting up

  1. Create an empty folder in your device's internal storage and name it.
  2. In the homepage of the site, click on "Load Folder". Your device will prompt you to use a folder. Select your folder.
  3. Once the folder is loaded, grant all required permissions on your call.
  4. Now you can go to "Add Topic", and add a topic of your choice. (Deletion isn't possible as of now, so choose wisely)
  5. Now when you click on load folder on the homepage, your topic will appear.
  6. Go to "Open Topic", and click on "Add Problems" to add a problem. (look below on this page for points to keep in mind)
  7. Once problem is added, you can click on "Load Folder" in your topic's page and your problem(s) will be loaded.
  8. You're good to go!


Adding a problem

Adding a problem is easy, but you might get confused. The "Relation Term" on the problems page is a random unique code you set for the system so that problems and solutions can be interconnected and retrieved. Eg- "Spheredisplavwm"


You can add tags, separated by spaces, so that you can filter out relevant problems by tags later. It is case SENSITIVE.
